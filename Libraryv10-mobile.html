AI features

Chapter names and summaries use Google's free Gemini API. Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/app/apikey (sign in, click Create API key), then paste it here. The key stays in this browser only. Short excerpts of the chapters are sent to Google, and on the free tier Google may use them to improve its products.

Google does not publish exact free limits. Your real ones are in AI Studio, so set the budget to whatever you want to stay under.
